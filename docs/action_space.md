# FlyBody Action Space: Complete Specification & Semantics

## 1. Overview & Verification
The action space for `flight_imitation` is a 12-dimensional continuous bounded vector:
* **Shape**: `(12,)` (`float64`)
* **Control Rate**: Updated at **5,000 Hz** ($\Delta t = 0.0002\text{ s}$)
* **Physics Integration**: 4 physics substeps occur per action update ($dt = 0.00005\text{ s}$, 20,000 Hz).

## 3. Semantics: What Are These 12 Values?

> **Question**: *Are the 12 values direct actuator commands, wingbeat modulation parameters, high-level control parameters, joint targets, or something else?*

### The Definitive Finding:
The 12 action channels are **HYBRID KINEMATIC MODULATION PARAMETERS AND DIRECT JOINT POSITION TARGETS**:

1. **Channels 0..2 (Head) & 9..10 (Abdomen)**:
   * **Nature**: **Direct Joint Position Targets** (Position Actuators).
   * **Mechanism**: In `FruitFly.apply_action()`, these values directly command target angles to proportional-derivative (PD) actuators on the head and abdominal hinges.
2. **Channels 3..8 (Wings)**:
   * **Nature**: **Kinematic Modulation Offsets (Biases)** added to the baseline cyclical wingbeat generated by the Wing Beat Pattern Generator (WBPG).
   * **Mechanism**: In `flight_imitation.py:before_step()`, the high-frequency 250 Hz baseline cyclical stroke pattern $\mathbf{q}_{wbpg}(t)$ is generated deterministically. The policy's 6 wing actions act as **kinematic offsets** $\Delta \mathbf{q}_{policy}$:
     $$\mathbf{q}_{desired}(t) = \mathbf{q}_{wbpg}(t) + \mathbf{a}_{3..8}$$
     This target is then converted to a virtual spring force:
     $$\mathbf{u}_{wings} = \mathbf{a}_{3..8} + (\mathbf{q}_{wbpg} - \mathbf{q}_{actual})$$
3. **Channel 11 (Flapping Frequency)**:
   * **Nature**: **Global Frequency Scaling Factor**.
   * **Mechanism**: Linearly scales the WBPG wingbeat flapping frequency between 196.2 Hz and 239.8 Hz around the mean baseline of 218 Hz:
     $$f_{stroke} = 218\text{ Hz} \times (1 + 0.10 \times a_{11})$$

### Why This Design Matters:
The learning agent **does NOT need to learn to flap its wings 250 times per second**. Flapping is handled by the biological pattern generator (Level 2). The agent only learns **how to steer, trim, bank, and throttle** by modulating stroke parameters at 5 kHz (Level 3).
